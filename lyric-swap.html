Lyric Swap

Upload a score with vocal lyrics and a spreadsheet of new words. You get the same sheet music back with the new lyrics printed under the same notes. Any line that doesn't fit cleanly stops and asks you first.

Sheet music Drop a PDF or image here, or click to choose PDF exported from MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico or Noteflight works best. Scans and photos (PDF, JPG, PNG) also work, with more checking.
New lyrics Drop a spreadsheet or document here, or click to choose CSV, Excel, Google Sheets export, Word or plain text. Best: one column with the original line and one with the new line.

Review lines

Let Claude fit the tricky lines.

Claude splits the new words into sung syllables and suggests how to fit lines that have too many or too few syllables. You approve every change. This uses your Claude usage.

Preview & download

Everything runs in your browser. Your files aren't uploaded anywhere unless you ask Claude for help, and then only the lyric text (or, for scans, images of the lyric lines) is sent.
